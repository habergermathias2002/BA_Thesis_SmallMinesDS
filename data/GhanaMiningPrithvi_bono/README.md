## Format

- `BONO_XXXX_IMG.tif`: 6 bands (B2,B3,B4,B8A,B11,B12), float32, DN = reflectance × 10 000
- `BONO_XXXX_MASK.tif`: 1 band uint8, 0=Non-Mining, 1=Mining  
  Mask includes **all** mining polygons intersecting the 128×128 window.
